# 🍩 FSD Dough

## Foundation Principles

The Dough represents the principles that guide FSD decisions.

For the project vision and identity, see:

`FSD-CONCEPT.md`

---

# Evolution Over Replacement

FSD values evolution.

Prefer:

- understanding existing behavior;
- improving incrementally;
- preserving valuable knowledge.

Avoid replacing working solutions without a clear reason.

---

# 🍩 Bake Small

Small changes are easier to:

- understand;
- review;
- test;
- improve.

Prefer:

- focused changes;
- incremental progress;
- clear objectives.

Avoid:

- unnecessary complexity;
- large uncontrolled changes;
- premature redesign.

---

# 🍩 Ship Often

Delivery creates feedback.

Prefer:

- frequent improvements;
- shorter development cycles;
- continuous validation.

Progress is created through iteration.

---

# 🍩 Keep Improving

Every change should improve the project.

Consider:

- maintainability;
- quality;
- usability;
- future evolution.

The goal is continuous improvement, not perfection before delivery.

---

# Simplicity First

Prefer solutions that are:

- clear;
- understandable;
- maintainable.

Avoid complexity without purpose.

---

# Preserve Knowledge

Existing systems contain valuable knowledge.

Important decisions should become reusable through:

- documentation;
- rules;
- conventions;
- project knowledge.

---

# Context Awareness

FSD should provide the right information at the right moment.

Prefer:

- focused context;
- reusable knowledge;
- efficient information usage.

Avoid unnecessary context and unnecessary complexity.

---

# Human Responsibility

FSD assists development.

Humans remain responsible for:

- decisions;
- validation;
- architecture;
- delivery.


---
🍩 Bake small.  
🍩 Ship often.  
🍩 Keep improving.
